Privacy Policy for Shero Baba: Lion Run

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Shero Baba: Lion Run ("the game") is an offline mobile game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game works without an internet connection and without an account.

Information we collect

None. The game has no sign-up, no login, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no third-party tracking tools. It does not send any data over the internet.

Information saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your phone only: coins, stars, upgrades, outfits, mission progress, settings (sound, vibration, language, on-screen buttons), your best scores, and the player name you choose on the Records screen. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in Android Settings deletes it permanently.

Permissions

  • Internet: a standard requirement of the app framework (Capacitor WebView). The game does not make network requests.
  • Vibration: short vibrations during play. You can switch this off in the game's Settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children. There are no ads, no purchases, no chat and no links out of the game.

Third parties

We do not share information with third parties because we have none to share. Google Play, which you use to download the game, has its own privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions? Email YOUR-EMAIL@example.com.
