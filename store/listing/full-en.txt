Meet Shero Baba, the bravest lion on Pakistan's roads!

Shero is on a journey from the Karachi sea front to the Margalla Hills of Islamabad. The roads are packed with painted trucks, green rickshaws, fruit carts and wedding banners. Swipe fast, keep running, and collect every golden coin on the way.

🦁 EASY TO PLAY, HARD TO PUT DOWN
• Swipe left or right to switch lanes
• Swipe up to jump over barriers and fruit carts
• Swipe down to slide under wedding banners
• Prefer buttons? Turn on on-screen controls in Settings

🚚 A RUN THROUGH PAKISTAN
Eight story stops, each with its own look:
• Karachi Sea View
• Multan Old City
• Trimmu Barrage
• Jhang Saddar
• Faisalabad Clock Tower
• Lahore Bazaar Nights
• Peshawar Qissa Khwani
• Islamabad Margalla Finale
Finish a stop to unlock the next one, then try the Endless Highway to chase your best score.

⭐ THREE STARS ON EVERY STOP
Earn one star for finishing, one for hitting the coin target, and one for a perfect run with no bumps.

🔥 ROAR POWER
Every coin fills your ROAR ring. When it glows, tap ROAR and Shero charges ahead, smashing trucks, rickshaws and barriers out of the way.

🧲 POWER-UPS
• Coin magnet pulls coins from every lane
• Shield saves you from one crash
• Double coins for a limited time

🔧 SHERO'S WORKSHOP
Spend your coins to make power-ups last longer and to dress Shero in a truck-art vest, a cricket kit, a white kameez, a royal sherwani and more.

🏆 MISSIONS AND RECORDS
Three new daily missions every day, twelve achievements, and your personal top 10 runs.

✅ MADE FOR EVERYONE
• Plays fully offline, no internet needed
• No sign-up, no account, no login
• No ads and no in-app purchases
• No personal data collected, progress stays on your phone
• English and Urdu (اردو)
• Works on Android 7.0 and newer

Tie your headband, Shero. The road is waiting!
